FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first Spect-IT test.

Is Spect-IT a replacement for an eye exam?
No. Spect-IT is a powerful screening and self-monitoring tool for informational use. It is not a diagnosis or a final prescription. For clinical decisions, always consult a licensed eye-care professional — and we help you find one nearby.
How accurate are the tests?
Our tests are clinically-inspired and use AI face-mapping to calibrate distance and conditions for consistency. Accuracy depends on your device, lighting and following the on-screen guidance — each test shows an accuracy indicator.
Do I need to create an account?
No sign-up is required to start testing. Creating an account lets you save results, track trends over time and unlock the premium analytics dashboard.
What do I need to take a test?
Just a device with a screen — phone, tablet or computer. Some tests use your camera for AI eye-tracking and face-mapping; camera access is always requested up front and processed on your device.
Can I use Spect-IT as an app?
Yes. On Android and desktop Chrome, tap Install app. On iPhone, open Spect-IT in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The installed app is this website in standalone mode — the same screening tests, shop enquiry, and specialist requests.
Is my data private?
Yes. Tests run in your browser and we treat your health data with care. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
