Astigmatism Test

Cover one eye and look at the center of the image. Do all lines appear equally dark and clear?

If some lines appear blurry, thicker, or darker than others, you may have astigmatism.

What to Look For:

  • All lines should appear equally sharp and dark
  • If vertical lines appear blurry: possible astigmatism with vertical axis
  • If horizontal lines appear blurry: possible astigmatism with horizontal axis
  • If diagonal lines appear blurry: possible astigmatism with oblique axis
