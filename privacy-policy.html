Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2024

1. Introduction

Spect-IT ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Test results and eye health measurements
  • Location data (when searching for nearby eye care professionals)
  • Photos you choose to save from the app

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, operating system)
  • App usage data
  • Camera data (processed locally on your device)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide eye health testing and measurement services
  • Generate test results and reports
  • Help you find nearby eye care professionals
  • Improve our app and services
  • Ensure app functionality and security

4. Data Storage and Security

All eye test processing is performed locally on your device. Test results and measurements are stored securely on your device. We do not transmit your eye test data to our servers unless you explicitly choose to share it.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Camera and Photo Library Access

Spect-IT requires camera access to perform eye tests and vision measurements. All camera processing is done locally on your device. We only access photos you explicitly choose to save from the app to your photo library.

6. Location Data

We use your location only when you actively search for nearby eye care professionals or retailers. Your location is used solely for this purpose and is not stored or shared with third parties.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to help us operate our app and analyze usage. These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of certain data collection
  • Request a copy of your data

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
