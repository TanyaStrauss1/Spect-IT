## After Build Appears

### Step 1: Wait for Processing

- Build status: "Processing"
- Time: 15-30 minutes typically
- You'll receive email at tanstrauss@gmail.com when ready

### Step 2: Build Ready

When status changes to "Ready to Test":

1. **Internal Testing:**
   - Go to "Internal Testing" tab
   - Add up to 100 internal testers
   - Instant access (no review needed)
   - Testers receive email invitation

2. **External Testing:**
   - Go to "External Testing" tab
   - Add up to 10,000 external testers
   - Requires App Review (24-48 hours)
   - Submit for review first

## Adding Testers

### Internal Testers (Recommended First)

1. Go to "Internal Testing" tab
2. Click "Add Testers" or "+"
3. Add email addresses:
   - tanstrauss@gmail.com (yourself)
   - Other team members
   - Up to 100 testers total
4. Testers receive email invitation
5. They install TestFlight app and accept invitation

### External Testers

1. Go to "External Testing" tab
2. Create a new group (if needed)
3. Add testers (up to 10,000)
4. Submit for App Review
5. Wait 24-48 hours for approval
6. Testers can then access the build

## Troubleshooting

### Build Not Appearing
- Wait 15-30 minutes for processing
- Check email at tanstrauss@gmail.com
- Verify upload completed in Xcode

### Build Stuck in Processing
- Normal processing: 15-30 minutes
- Can take up to 1 hour during peak times
- Check App Store Connect status page

### Testers Not Receiving Invitations
- Check spam folder
- Verify email addresses are correct
- Resend invitations if needed
