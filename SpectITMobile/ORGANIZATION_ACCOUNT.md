# 🏢 Convert to Organization Account?

## ❓ What This Means

Expo is asking if you want to convert your personal account into an organization account.

---

## ✅ Benefits of Organization

- **Team Collaboration:** Multiple team members can access
- **Business Features:** Better for company/business apps
- **Shared Resources:** Team billing and shared quotas
- **Permissions:** Granular access control for team members

---

## ❌ Downsides

- **More Complex:** More setup and management
- **Not Needed for Solo:** Usually unnecessary for individual developers
- **Can Change Later:** You can always convert later if needed

---

## 💡 Recommendation

### For Your Situation: **SKIP or NO**

**Reasons:**
- You're working solo (no team members)
- Personal account works perfectly fine
- Can convert later if you need team features
- Simpler to start with personal account
- All build features work the same

---

## ✅ What to Do

**Click one of these:**
- "Skip"
- "No"
- "Not Now"
- "Maybe Later"

You can always convert to organization later from account settings if you need team features.

---

## 📋 After Skipping

Continue with your account setup:
1. Complete account verification
2. Login to EAS: `eas login`
3. Build iOS app: `./build_with_tanstrauss.exp`

---

## 🔄 Convert Later (If Needed)

If you later need organization features:
1. Go to: https://expo.dev/accounts/[your-username]/settings
2. Find "Convert to Organization" option
3. Follow the conversion process

---

## 💡 Key Points

- **Personal account = Full functionality** for solo developers
- **Organization = Team features** (only needed if you have a team)
- **You can build and deploy** with either account type
- **No difference** in build capabilities

---

**Bottom Line: Skip for now, convert later if you need team features!**
