# 🔓 Unlock Apple Developer Portal Access

## ⚠️ Issue

You can sign in to your Apple ID, but the **Apple Developer Portal** still shows the account as locked.

This happens because:
- Regular Apple ID unlock ≠ Developer Portal unlock
- Developer services need separate verification
- There can be a delay in unlock propagation

---

## 🔓 Solution: Sign In to Developer Portal

### Step 1: Go to Developer Portal

1. **Open:** https://developer.apple.com/account
2. **Sign in with:** `tanstrauss@gmail.com`
3. **Enter your password**

### Step 2: Complete Verification

If prompted:
- **Enter verification code** (from your phone or email)
- **Answer security questions**
- **Complete any additional verification**

### Step 3: Verify Access

Once signed in, you should see:
- Your developer account dashboard
- Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles
- App Store Connect access
