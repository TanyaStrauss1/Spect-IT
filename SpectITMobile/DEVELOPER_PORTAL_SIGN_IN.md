# 🔐 Apple Developer Portal Sign-In Guide

## 📋 What You Should See

When you visit [https://developer.apple.com/account](https://developer.apple.com/account), you'll see one of these:

---

## ✅ Option 1: Sign In Button

If you see a **"Sign In"** button or sign-in form:

### Steps:

1. **Click "Sign In"** (if there's a button)
2. **Enter Apple ID:** `tanstrauss@gmail.com`
3. **Enter your password**
4. **Complete verification** if prompted:
   - Enter verification code from your phone
   - Or answer security questions
5. **You should see your developer account dashboard**

---

## ✅ Option 2: Already Signed In

If you see your **developer account dashboard** with:

- **Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles**
- **App Store Connect** link
- **Membership details**
- **Your account information**

**This means you're successfully signed in and unlocked! ✅**

---

## ❌ Option 3: Still Locked Message

If you see:

- **"Account locked"** message
- **Error -20209**
- **"This Apple Account has been locked"**

**This means:**
- The unlock may still be propagating (wait 5-15 minutes)
- Or you need additional verification
- Or contact Apple Support

---

## 📝 What to Tell Me

After trying to sign in, tell me:

### ✅ Success:
- **"I can see the developer dashboard"** → I'll retry the iOS build immediately

### ❌ Still Issues:
- **"Still locked"** → We'll wait or contact support
- **"Need to sign in"** → Follow the sign-in steps above
- **"Can't access"** → We'll troubleshoot further
