# Submit Spect-IT to App Store

## Complete App Store Submission Guide

### Step 1: Prepare App Information

#### Required Information

1. **App Name:**
   - Display Name: Spect-IT
   - Subtitle: (Optional) Professional Eye Testing

2. **Category:**
   - Primary: Medical
   - Secondary: Health & Fitness

3. **Age Rating:**
   - Complete age rating questionnaire
   - Medical apps typically: 4+ or 12+

4. **Description:**
   ```
   Spect-IT is a professional-grade vision assessment app that transforms 
   your device into a comprehensive eye testing platform. 
   
   Features:
   - AI-powered vision tests
   - 3D face mapping
   - Advanced eye tracking
   - Comprehensive analytics
   - Test history and progress tracking
   - Find nearby eye care specialists
   - Shop eyewear products
   
   Monitor your eye health, measure prescriptions, and track changes 
   over time. Share results with your eye care professional.
   ```

5. **Keywords:**
   - eye test, vision test, eye health, optometry, eye exam, 
     prescription, visual acuity, color blindness, eye care

6. **Support URL:**
   - https://spect-it.com (or your support page)

7. **Marketing URL:** (Optional)
   - https://spect-it.com

8. **Privacy Policy URL:**
   - Required for medical apps
   - https://spect-it.com/privacy (or your privacy policy page)

### Step 8: Advertising Identifier (IDFA)

1. **Does your app use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA)?**
   - Select "No" (unless you're using ads)
   - If yes, complete IDFA questionnaire

### Step 9: Submit for Review

1. **Review All Information:**
   - Verify all required fields are complete
   - Check screenshots are uploaded
   - Verify build is selected
   - Review app description and keywords

2. **Submit:**
   - Click "Submit for Review" button
   - Confirm submission
   - Status changes to "Waiting for Review"

### Step 10: Review Process

**Timeline:**
- Initial review: 24-48 hours typically
- Can take up to 7 days during peak times
- You'll receive email updates at tanstrauss@gmail.com

**Possible Outcomes:**
- ✅ **Approved** - App goes live
- ⚠️ **Rejected** - Review feedback provided, fix issues and resubmit
- 📧 **More Information Needed** - Respond to reviewer questions
