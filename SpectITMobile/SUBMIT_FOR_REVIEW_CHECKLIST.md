## ⏱️ Review Timeline

**Typical Review Time:**
- **First submission:** 1-3 days
- **Updates:** 1-2 days
- **Rejections:** Usually same day (if issues found)

**Status Updates:**
- "Waiting for Review" - In queue
- "In Review" - Being reviewed
- "Pending Developer Release" - Approved, waiting for you
- "Ready for Sale" - Live on App Store
- "Rejected" - Issues found (check email for details)

---

## 📧 What Happens Next

### After Submission:

1. **Email Confirmation:**
   - You'll receive email confirming submission
   - Check spam folder if not received

2. **Review Process:**
   - Apple reviews your app
   - Usually takes 1-3 days
   - You'll receive email when status changes

3. **If Approved:**
   - Status: "Pending Developer Release"
   - You can release immediately or schedule release
   - App goes live on App Store

4. **If Rejected:**
   - Status: "Rejected"
   - Email explains why
   - Fix issues and resubmit

---

## ⚠️ Common Rejection Reasons

1. **Missing Information:**
   - Incomplete app description
   - Missing screenshots
   - Missing privacy policy

2. **Privacy Issues:**
   - Privacy policy not accessible
   - Missing privacy disclosures
   - Data collection not disclosed

3. **Functionality:**
   - App crashes
   - Features don't work
   - Broken links

4. **Guidelines:**
   - Violates App Store guidelines
   - Misleading information
   - Inappropriate content
