# How to Submit Spect-IT to App Store - Step by Step

## Complete Submission Guide

### Step 1: App Information Tab

1. **Go to:** App Information tab in App Store Connect
2. **Complete these fields:**

   - **Name:** Spect-IT
   - **Subtitle:** (Optional) Professional Eye Testing
   - **Primary Language:** English
   - **Category:**
     - Primary: Medical
     - Secondary: Health & Fitness
   - **Age Rating:** Click "Edit" and complete questionnaire
   - **Description:** (See copy/paste below)
   - **Keywords:** eye test, vision test, eye health, optometry, eye exam, prescription, visual acuity, color blindness, eye care
   - **Support URL:** https://tanyastrauss1.github.io/Spect-IT/
   - **Marketing URL:** (Optional) https://tanyastrauss1.github.io/Spect-IT/
   - **Privacy Policy URL:** https://tanyastrauss1.github.io/Spect-IT/privacy-policy.html

3. **Click "Save"**

### Step 2: Pricing and Availability

1. **Go to:** Pricing and Availability tab
2. **Set:**
   - **Price:** Free (or select paid tier)
   - **Availability:** All countries (or select specific countries)
   - **Availability Date:** Today's date (or future date)
3. **Click "Save"**

### Step 3: App Store Tab - Version Information

#### 3.1 Screenshots (Required)

1. **Go to:** App Store tab
2. **Scroll to Screenshots section**
3. **Upload screenshots:**
   - **Minimum required:** iPhone 6.7" display (1290 x 2796 pixels)
   - **Optional:** Other iPhone sizes
   - **Optional:** iPad sizes (if supporting iPad)

**Screenshot Requirements:**
- Format: PNG or JPEG
- Size: 1290 x 2796 pixels (iPhone 6.7")
- Show key features of your app
- At least 1 screenshot required

#### 3.2 Build Selection (CRITICAL)

1. **Scroll to "Build" section**
2. **Click "+" or "Select a build before you submit your app"**
3. **Select your build:**
   - Version: 1.0
   - Build: 1
   - Status: "Ready to Submit" or "Ready to Test"
4. **Build should appear in the Build field**

#### 3.3 Version Information

1. **Version:** 1.0
2. **What's New in This Version:**
   ```
   Initial release of Spect-IT
   
   - Professional-grade vision assessment
   - AI-powered eye testing
   - Comprehensive test results and analytics
   - Find nearby eye care specialists
   - Track your eye health over time
   ```
3. **Promotional Text:** (Optional) Short marketing text
4. **Click "Save"**

### Step 4: App Review Information

1. **Scroll to:** App Review Information section
2. **Complete:**
   - **First Name:** Tanya
   - **Last Name:** Strauss
   - **Phone Number:** (Your phone number)
   - **Email:** tanstrauss@gmail.com
   - **Demo Account:** (If app requires login - leave blank if not needed)

3. **Click "Save"**

### Step 5: Version Release

1. **Scroll to:** Version Release section
2. **Select:**
   - **Automatically release this version** (Recommended)
   - Or: **Manually release this version**
3. **Click "Save"**

### Step 6: Export Compliance

1. **Scroll to:** Export Compliance section
2. **Answer:**
   - **Does your app use encryption?**
     - Select "No" (unless you use custom encryption)
     - Or "Yes" and provide compliance information
3. **Click "Save"**

### Step 7: Advertising Identifier (IDFA)

1. **Scroll to:** Advertising Identifier section
2. **Answer:**
   - **Does your app use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA)?**
     - Select "No" (unless you're using ads)
     - If yes, complete IDFA questionnaire
3. **Click "Save"**

### Step 8: Submit for Review

**Before submitting, verify:**
- [ ] All required fields completed
- [ ] Screenshots uploaded (at least 1 set)
- [ ] Build selected and visible
- [ ] Privacy Policy URL accessible
- [ ] Support URL accessible
- [ ] Description and keywords added
- [ ] Age rating completed

**Submit:**
1. **Scroll to top of page**
2. **Click "Submit for Review" button** (top right)
3. **Review the summary**
4. **Click "Submit" to confirm**
5. **Status changes to "Waiting for Review"**

## Review Process

### Timeline
- **Initial Review:** 24-48 hours typically
- **Can take up to:** 7 days during peak times
- **Email Updates:** tanstrauss@gmail.com

### Status Updates
- **Waiting for Review** → Submitted, waiting
- **In Review** → Being reviewed
- **Ready for Sale** → Approved, app goes live
- **Rejected** → Review feedback provided

### After Submission

1. **Monitor Status:**
   - Check App Store Connect regularly
   - Check email at tanstrauss@gmail.com

2. **If Approved:**
   - App goes live automatically (if set to auto-release)
   - Available in App Store worldwide
   - Email notification sent

3. **If Rejected:**
   - Review feedback provided
   - Fix issues
   - Resubmit
