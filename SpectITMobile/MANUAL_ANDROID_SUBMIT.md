   - Sign in with your Google account

2. **Create App (if not created):**
   - Click **"Create app"** or **"All apps"** → **"Create app"**
   - Fill in:
     - **App name:** `Spect-IT`
     - **Default language:** English
     - **App or game:** App
     - **Free or paid:** Free
     - **Declarations:** Check required boxes
   - Click **"Create app"**

3. **Upload the .aab File:**
   - Go to **"Production"** (or **"Testing"** → **"Internal testing"**)
   - Click **"Create new release"**
   - Under **"App bundles"**, click **"Upload"**
   - Select the `.aab` file you downloaded
   - Wait for upload to complete

4. **Complete Release Details:**
   - **Release name:** `1.0.0` (or version number)

     ```
     - Prescription measurement
     - Find nearby eye care professionals
     ```
