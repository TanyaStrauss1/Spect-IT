# 🎯 Easiest Way to Create App in App Store Connect

## ✅ **EASIEST METHOD: App Store Connect Web Interface**

The **web interface** is the easiest and most straightforward way to create your app.

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step (Takes 2 minutes)

### Step 1: Go to App Store Connect

**Direct Link:** https://appstoreconnect.apple.com

1. Open your web browser
2. Go to: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
3. Sign in with your Apple ID:
   - Email: `tanstrauss@gmail.com` (or your Apple ID)
   - Password: [Your password]

---

### Step 2: Create New App

1. Click **"My Apps"** (top left)
2. Click the **"+"** button (top left, next to "My Apps")
3. Click **"New App"**

---

### Step 3: Fill in the Form

**This is a simple form with 5 fields:**

| Field | Value to Enter |
|-------|----------------|
| **Platform** | Select: **iOS** |
| **Name** | `Spect-IT` |
| **Primary Language** | Select: **English (U.S.)** |
| **Bundle ID** | Select: `com.spectit.app` (must be registered first) |
| **SKU** | `spectit-mobile-001` |
| **User Access** | Select: **Full Access** |

---

### Step 4: Click "Create"

That's it! Your app is created.

---

## ⚠️ Important: Register Bundle ID First

**Before creating the app, you MUST register the Bundle ID:**

### Quick Bundle ID Registration:

1. Go to: https://developer.apple.com/account/resources/identifiers/list
2. Click **"+"** button (top left)
3. Select **"App IDs"** → Click **"Continue"**
4. Select **"App"** → Click **"Continue"**
5. Fill in:
   - **Description:** `Spect-IT App`
   - **Bundle ID:** Select **"Explicit"** → Enter: `com.spectit.app`
6. Select capabilities:
   - ✅ Camera
   - ✅ Location Services
   - ✅ Photo Library
7. Click **"Continue"** → **"Register"**

**Takes 1 minute!**
