## ✅ Step 1: Complete the Purchase

### On the Apple Developer Portal:

1. **Click:** "Purchase" or "Complete Purchase" button
2. **Enter payment information:**

   - Billing address
   - Payment method details
3. **Review and confirm:**

   - Auto-renewal: Enabled (can be disabled later)

4. **Submit payment**

- Sets up your App Store Connect access
- Enables certificate and provisioning profile creation

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "Payment Failed"

- Check credit card details
- Verify billing address matches card
- Contact your bank if card is declined
- Try a different payment method

### "Still Processing After 48 Hours"

- Check spam/junk folder for emails
- Go to: https://developer.apple.com/account
- Check enrollment status
- Contact Apple Developer Support if needed

### "Can't Access After Approval"

- Clear browser cache
- Try incognito/private browsing
- Sign out and sign back in
- Contact Apple Support
